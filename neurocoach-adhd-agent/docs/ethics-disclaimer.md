# 🔒 NeuroCoach Ethics & Safety Statement

## Purpose
This document outlines the ethical considerations, safety measures, and responsible AI practices implemented in NeuroCoach to ensure user safety, clinical appropriateness, and legal compliance.

## Core Ethical Principles

### 1. Not a Diagnostic Tool
**NeuroCoach is explicitly designed as an educational and self-coaching aid, NOT a diagnostic or medical service.**

- Clear disclaimers appear on every page and interaction
- Users are repeatedly informed that screening results are not clinical diagnoses
- Professional medical consultation is always recommended

### 2. User Autonomy & Consent
- Users maintain full control over their data and experience
- Informed consent is obtained before any data collection
- Users can delete their data and discontinue use at any time
- No coercive or manipulative design patterns

### 3. Transparency & Explainability
- AI decision-making processes are transparent to users
- Users understand what data is collected and how it's used
- Clear explanations of AI limitations and capabilities
- Open communication about system confidence levels

## Safety Measures

### 1. Clinical Boundaries
- **No Diagnosis**: System never claims to diagnose ADHD or any condition
- **No Treatment**: System does not provide medical treatment or therapy
- **Professional Referral**: Always directs users to qualified healthcare providers
- **Scope Limitation**: Clearly defined boundaries of AI assistance

### 2. Crisis Prevention & Response
- **Sentiment Analysis**: Monitors user messages for signs of distress
- **Crisis Resources**: Immediate access to mental health crisis lines
- **Escalation Protocols**: Clear pathways for professional intervention
- **Safety Keywords**: Triggers for additional support resources

### 3. Data Privacy & Security
- **HIPAA Alignment**: Data handling follows healthcare privacy standards
- **Encryption**: All data encrypted in transit and at rest
- **Minimal Collection**: Only necessary data collected for functionality
- **User Control**: Complete data deletion and export capabilities

## User Protection Features
