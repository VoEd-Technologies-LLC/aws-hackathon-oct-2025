# 🎬 NeuroCoach Demo Script - 3 Minute Presentation

## Demo Overview
This script provides a complete walkthrough for the 3-minute NeuroCoach demonstration, designed to maximize impact across all hackathon judging criteria. The demo showcases the complete user journey from ADHD screening to personalized coaching and therapy preparation.

## Timing Breakdown
- **0:00-0:15**: Problem introduction and solution overview
- **0:15-0:45**: ASRS screening demonstration
- **0:45-1:15**: Results interpretation and subtype explanation
- **1:15-2:15**: Interactive coaching session with personalized strategies
- **2:15-2:45**: Therapy preparation and resource integration
- **2:45-3:00**: Impact statement and technical architecture highlight

## Complete Script

### 🎯 Introduction (0:00-0:15)
**"Good [morning/afternoon], judges. Today I'm excited to introduce NeuroCoach - an AWS-powered AI agent that revolutionizes ADHD self-coaching and therapy preparation.**

**ADHD affects over 360 million people worldwide, yet millions remain undiagnosed or struggle with self-management. Traditional tools offer only static questionnaires with generic advice, leaving individuals without personalized strategies or guidance for effective therapy sessions.**

**NeuroCoach bridges this critical gap by combining clinical screening with interactive AI coaching, empowering users with tailored strategies while preparing them for productive conversations with human therapists."**

### 📊 ASRS Screening (0:15-0:45)
**"Let me show you how NeuroCoach works. We'll start with the WHO-validated ASRS v1.1 screening questionnaire.**

*[Navigate to screening page and begin questionnaire]*

**The system presents the 6-question ASRS screener in an intuitive, accessible format. Users respond to questions about their attention and hyperactivity challenges over the past 6 months.**

*[Complete sample responses:]*

1. **"How often do you have trouble wrapping up the final details of a project?"** - Often
2. **"How often do you have difficulty getting things in order when you have to do a task that requires organization?"** - Often
3. **"How often do you have problems remembering appointments or obligations?"** - Very Often

*[Continue with remaining questions, showing progress indicator]*

**The interface is designed with ADHD users in mind - clear, focused, and accessible with WCAG 2.1 AA compliance."**

### 📈 Results & Analysis (0:45-1:15)
**"Once completed, NeuroCoach automatically scores the ASRS and determines the probable ADHD subtype.**

*[Show results page]*

**Based on the responses, the system identifies this as likely inattentive ADHD presentation and provides clear, understandable interpretation:**

*'Your screening suggests characteristics commonly associated with inattentive ADHD. This includes challenges with focus, organization, and task completion. Remember, this is an educational tool - please consult with a healthcare professional for proper evaluation.'*

**The agent explains results in plain language, avoiding clinical jargon while providing meaningful context."**

### 🎯 Interactive Coaching (1:15-2:15)
**"Now comes the real power of NeuroCoach - personalized coaching based on individual challenges.**

*[Navigate to coaching interface]*

**User: 'I keep missing deadlines at work and it's affecting my performance.'**

**NeuroCoach: 'This is a very common challenge with inattentive ADHD. Based on your screening results, here are three tailored strategies that may help:**

1. **External Accountability**: Consider using a body double - work alongside a colleague or use virtual co-working sessions to maintain focus and momentum.

2. **Visual Progress Tracking**: Create a visual project board or use a task management app with clear progress indicators. Breaking tasks into smaller, visible steps can help maintain motivation.

3. **Time Blocking with Buffers**: Schedule focused work periods of 25-45 minutes, followed by short breaks. Add buffer time between tasks to account for transitions and unexpected delays.

**Would you like me to elaborate on any of these strategies or help you adapt them to your specific work environment?'**

*[Show user asking follow-up question]*

**User: 'The time blocking sounds good, but I get distracted during the focused periods.'**

**NeuroCoach: 'That's a great observation. For managing distractions during focused work:*

- **Environment Setup**: Create a dedicated workspace with minimal visual distractions
- **Digital Boundaries**: Use website blockers during focus periods and put your phone in another room
- **Sensory Considerations**: Consider noise-cancelling headphones or background sounds that help you focus

**For your therapy sessions, you might discuss: 'What workplace accommodations might help me maintain focus during structured work periods?'**

**The agent provides specific talking points tailored to the user's situation, bridging digital coaching with human therapy."**

### 🩺 Therapy Preparation & Resources (2:15-2:45)
**"NeuroCoach also prepares users for effective therapy sessions and connects them with valuable resources.**

*[Show therapy preparation section]*

**'Based on our conversation about work deadlines, here are specific points to discuss with your therapist:*

1. **Workplace Impact**: How ADHD symptoms are affecting your job performance and career progression
2. **Accommodation Strategies**: What reasonable accommodations might help in your work environment
3. **Coping Skill Development**: Building sustainable strategies for task initiation and completion
4. **Progress Tracking**: How to measure improvement and adjust strategies over time

*[Navigate to resource library]*

**The system also provides curated resources from reputable organizations like CHADD and NIMH, plus downloadable coping strategy guides and productivity tools."**

### 🏆 Impact & Technical Excellence (2:45-3:00)
**"NeuroCoach represents a breakthrough in accessible ADHD support - combining clinical validation with personalized AI coaching.**

**Built on AWS Bedrock AgentCore with Lambda, DynamoDB, and S3, the system provides:**
- **Scalable Architecture**: Serverless design supporting thousands of users
- **Security-First**: HIPAA-aligned data handling with comprehensive encryption
- **Ethical AI**: Transparent boundaries with professional referral pathways
- **Global Impact**: Accessible support for underserved populations worldwide

**By addressing the critical gap between ADHD awareness and effective self-management, NeuroCoach empowers individuals while respecting clinical boundaries and promoting professional care.**

**Thank you for your attention. NeuroCoach - transforming ADHD self-coaching through the power of AWS AI."**
